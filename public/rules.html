RDC Community Rules and Safety

Community rules updated October 21, 2026. Reference: rdc-community-2026-10-21-vip. Marketplace confirmation wording and agreement versions are unchanged.

Be respectful. Be honest. Share only what you may share. These rules cover RDC channels, resources, marketplace posts, partnerships and activity handled by RU. They also cover deals and misconduct meaningfully connected to RDC, even if they continue in DMs or elsewhere. They do not claim control over unrelated private activity.

RDC is an independent community, not Roblox or Discord. A listing, staff badge, partner badge, Contributor role or bot message is not proof of ownership, an endorsement or a promise of a safe deal.

1. Keep the community safe

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, raids, sexual content, doxxing or sharing private information without a lawful reason. Keep the community safe for younger members.
  • No scams, impersonation, fake reviews, dishonest portfolios, stolen accounts, spam, mass mentions or unwanted promotional DMs. Do not pressure members for money or private information.
  • No malware, backdoors, credential theft, exploit tools or instructions meant to compromise someone else's account, game or device. Files shared here are not guaranteed to be scanned or safe.
  • Use the correct channels. Do not evade moderation, use alternate accounts to bypass restrictions or retaliate against people who report concerns in good faith.
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including its age requirements. Follow relevant platform rules and the law.

2. Respect creators and ownership

Official VIP releases are separate from ordinary member sharing in #resources. The owner and RDC bot initially publish official VIP previews/resources; members may ask questions beneath posts and VIP members may reply in private resource posts. Replies and support attachments are not official reviewed releases. Ordinary #resources posting, Contributor features and existing VIP role/tag/manual/grace arrangements remain unchanged. Do not use an official post as permission to redistribute its files.

You are responsible for having the rights, permissions or other lawful basis needed for what you upload, display, distribute, license or sell. This includes other people's material inside a project. Credit is not permission. Buying an asset or having permission to use it does not automatically permit sharing its files, resale or sublicensing.

Do not post stolen, leaked, ripped, decompiled or private commissioned material without the necessary authorization. Describe your part in collaborative work honestly. Follow license limits and required attribution. Do not claim ownership of someone else's work.

RDC may investigate and restrict or remove content that breaks these rules. A report is an allegation to check, not an automatic finding of infringement. Community moderation is not a court deciding copyright ownership.

3. Post and make deals clearly

For Hiring, For Hire or Selling, go to #market-info, click Create Listing and fill in the form. Add pictures if needed and choose your tags. Check your private preview, make any changes, read the agreement, then click I Agree & Send to Staff. Staff review that exact version before publication. You can also start with /apply and return with /pending. DMs are optional. Normal messages in #market-info do not submit a listing.

Give an honest description, price or budget, payment method, delivery expectations, contact details and important limits. For hiring, rights requirements apply to material you provide, not work you have not yet commissioned. For sales, explain what the buyer receives and may do with it. For services, show an honest portfolio and your own contribution.

Before paying or starting work, open the listing's private deal ticket. Agree in writing on scope, rights, price, payment schedule, deadline, revisions and cancellation or refund terms. Participants and authorized server staff can read that ticket. Keep confidential complaints and unnecessary personal/payment details out of it.

RDC does not handle marketplace payments. Tickets are not escrow, insurance or payment protection. Approval does not prove ownership or guarantee payment, delivery, refunds or a safe deal. Check the other person and their permissions yourself. Do not send passwords, account tokens or payment credentials. Fraudulent payment reversals are prohibited; legitimate disputes and legal rights are not removed.

The actual poster's agreement and approval from a different authorized staff member must cover the same listing version. Important changes need both again. Unfinished drafts expire after 24 hours; editing or opening a ticket does not extend that deadline. Listings already waiting for staff review do not expire automatically. Older posts do not gain an agreement record just because these instructions changed.

4. Resources stay simple

Post normally in public #resources. No mandatory form, confirmation, private intake or staff preapproval is required there. Contributor features stay in place, but participation and rewards are not ownership checks. Official VIP releases are the separate owner/bot-published catalogue; no member self-service publishing is introduced.

Say what the resource does and how it may be used. For other creators' material, follow the license and include the required source and credit. Avoid misleading download links, hidden harmful files and unrelated ads. You remain responsible for the rights needed to distribute everything included.

5. Promotions, partnerships and rewards

Owner-approved official VIP previews and, when enabled, one small silent Resources navigation reply are authorized discovery placements. They do not authorize member DMs, role pings, extra announcements or bumps for each release. Disclose paid or gifted arrangements when relevant; no promotion itself proves ownership, redistribution authority or safety.

Anyone who can use the server may post normally in self-promo, subject to normal timeouts and safety restrictions. Keep promotions and invites in self-promo or authorized partner posts. Relevant listing, portfolio and product links belong in marketplace posts. Relevant downloads, sources and licenses belong in resources. Deal, help and evidence links belong in the appropriate private ticket or staff workflow. Ordinary useful discussion is not automatically an advertisement.

For a partnership, open a support ticket with your community or project, link and proposed exchange. Member count is not the deciding factor: a welcoming active community, a developing project or another useful collaboration may be a good fit. Both sides must agree to placements, duration, payments and pings. No fake activity, forced mentions or unwanted DMs. Posting alone does not create an official partnership. Only authorized people may commit RDC.

Clearly disclose paid, gifted, referral or otherwise rewarded recommendations where relevant. Partner status is not a guarantee. Existing partner bumps remain limited to the recorded post owner once every 24 hours. Giveaways and rewards must describe their actual conditions honestly; no invented prizes or misleading odds.

6. Report privately

Safety, copyright, privacy and staff concerns: rdc.safety@proton.me. The RDC owner monitors this address, including when RU is offline or you cannot access the server. No Discord DM or membership is required. It is not an emergency service.

When RU's private reporting service is available, use Report to RDC from a message's Apps menu or /safety report. Use /safety my-cases to open your private status, permitted follow-ups and available appeal controls. /safety inbox is an alternative for a specific case. Only the owner currently handles confidential RDC cases; ordinary staff roles do not grant access. DMs are optional. Private status is something you can return to, not a guaranteed alert. If the service is unavailable, email rdc.safety@proton.me.

Send the post/message link, a short explanation and only the evidence needed. Redact unrelated personal information. Do not send passwords, tokens, government IDs, card/bank details or full financial records. Do not put private allegations in a public post or ordinary deal ticket. For immediate danger or platform violations, use the appropriate emergency or platform reporting route without waiting for RDC.

Good-faith reports may be mistaken. Do not punish someone just for making one. Knowingly false evidence, malicious reports and retaliation may be investigated separately. Staff should promptly assess credible concerns, preserve only needed evidence and use an appropriate authorized restriction or removal where warranted. Archiving or locking a public forum post does not hide it. Removal cannot recall downloaded copies or control other platforms.

Confirmed violations may lead to warnings, posting restrictions, content removal or loss of access, depending on severity, history and the evidence. Severe abuse may require urgent protective action under existing moderation authority. The number of reports alone is not a finding. Repeated confirmed copyright violations may lead to removal of posting privileges or server access in appropriate circumstances; overturned findings must not count.

7. Reviews, appeals and legal requests

To challenge an RDC decision, email the same private address with the decision or post reference, what you believe is wrong and relevant new information. A person involved in a complaint must not decide it, and the original decision-maker must not decide their own appeal.

Currently, only the owner is designated for new private safety cases. Independent review of that person's own decision, conduct or conflict is not available until another eligible reviewer is appointed. Do not describe owner self-review as independent. Do not wait for an internal review if a platform or legal deadline applies.

Formal copyright notices, counter-notices and other legal correspondence are separate from community appeals. Use the relevant platform procedures and contact RDC's operator through the mailbox where RDC action is needed. Do not post legally required identifying information publicly. The mailbox is not represented as a registered DMCA agent. Nothing here claims that RDC automatically qualifies for a copyright safe harbor or replaces applicable legal procedures.

8. Staff access and accountability

Staff must use only authorized access, limit confidential information to those who need it, disclose personal or paid interests, step away from conflicts and record the basis and actual outcome of actions. A badge alone does not authorize holding member money, guaranteeing deals, promising refunds, signing contracts for RDC or giving legal advice.

Members and staff may report mistakes, pressure, harassment or misuse privately. Correct mistakes where possible and keep an honest audit record. Do not hide evidence, retaliate or use powers for personal disputes. Improper staff instructions do not override these rules or anyone's legal rights. Misuse may lead to removal of access and further action. Staff privacy matters too: do not publicly expose their personal information.

Independent approval requirements in the case system are not bypassed by calling an action urgent. Its emergency-removal feature remains disabled without the required independent follow-up. Existing moderation powers are not a substitute for independent case review.

9. Older posts and outside services

Existing posts can still be reported and checked under applicable rights, safety rules and platform requirements. Being old, approved, imported or bot-authored does not prove permission or ownership. New agreement records apply to the version actually agreed to; they are not fabricated for past posts. Cosmetic edits do not certify old claims or change their authorship.

Rocquire is independently operated. RDC can address RDC-side activity and use an authorized, limited referral; it cannot promise Rocquire removal, refunds or investigations. Official imports governed by Rocquire's own website procedures must not receive fabricated duplicate consent. Sharing private evidence requires an appropriate basis and restricted handling, not an automatic transfer.

10. Privacy, changes and limits

The Privacy Policy explains what RU stores, access, requests and backup limits. Not every legacy record is encrypted or covered by an automatic deletion schedule. The owner-approved targets for the prepared private-case service are 90 days after closure for evidence and 365 days for finding details, with active-case review and pauses for appeals or legal preservation. These targets are not active until the service and recovery safeguards are enabled. Discord and other people's copies are separate.

These rules describe community expectations and practical protections. They do not waive rights that cannot legally be waived, excuse staff misconduct or eliminate RDC's, its operator's or staff members' legal responsibilities. Changes should be clearly communicated. Material agreement changes need a new version rather than silently changing an accepted record.
