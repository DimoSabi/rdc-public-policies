RU SIGMA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026; VIP release and delivery records.

RDC Community Rules and Safety · Bot Terms

RU SIGMA is the privately operated Discord bot for Roblox Dev Club (RDC). The operator is the RDC server owner, Discord username internetlord._ (CEO OF THE INTERNET). This policy covers the bot and the owner's connected administration tools, not Discord's own services.

Data we process

  • Discord user, server, role, channel, message and thread IDs; member roles and server-tag state; timestamps and workflow status.
  • Application and marketplace answers, voluntarily submitted or forwarded listing text, embeds, component text, contact links, source identifiers, and uploaded images/files. Imported source snapshots and revision records may be preserved for review and duplicate detection.
  • Contributor-post metadata, giveaway participation/activity counters, role eligibility and reward state, moderation actions, warnings, and security audit records.
  • Configured message/attachment content when needed for moderation, intake, resource rewards, prefix commands or announcement publishing. Owner tools also collect selected channel structure, public pins/forum-post metadata and manually captured invite/Insights statistics. These are not a complete archive of every server conversation.
  • Optional feature records may include consent/button interactions and private thread identifiers. Disabling a feature does not necessarily erase its historical records.

Why we use it

To run applications and staff review, publish and manage listings, prevent duplicates, award configured roles, run giveaways, moderate abuse, enforce staff permissions, maintain accountability, and recover workflows after restarts. We do not sell Discord API data or use message content to train AI or machine-learning models.

For Roblox News, RU checks configured official public DevForum sources and saves source text, links, versions, classification, review decisions and delivery history. These records support accurate source-linked notices, change detection, duplicate prevention and restart recovery. It does not copy source images or collect forum replies for this feature. RDC is independent of Roblox.

When private Trust & Safety reporting is enabled, it also processes report details, permitted evidence, case participants and assignments, staff decisions, appeals, preservation holds and minimal audit records. Marketplace agreement records identify the poster, the displayed rules and the exact listing version. Do not send passwords, tokens, government IDs or full bank/card records. Disabled features are not available merely because they are described here.

Storage, access and sharing

Hosting is being moved from Railway to an owner-managed Windows computer. The former Railway deployment has been stopped; its original persistent volume remains in restricted recovery custody while the migration is reconciled. Records and submitted files are stored in SQLite. Authorized owner/staff access is limited by feature permissions. Published listings and announcements are visible to their intended Discord audiences; announcements can be distributed to servers following the announcement channel. Discord processes platform data, and the former hosting provider may retain provider-side copies. The owner may retain restricted recovery copies and local dashboard projections. Operational monitoring, when configured, receives health signals rather than submitted listing/application content. Secrets and private databases are not part of this public website.

The SQLite application does not independently encrypt every database field. When configured, rotating recovery archives use separate AES-256 ZIP encryption. Windows account protection restricts locally saved credentials; portable recovery credentials require a separate transfer passphrase. A backup on the same computer is not an independent off-host copy. Hosting-provider storage encryption does not protect exported copies. Replacing a credential or adding a new encryption key does not re-encrypt older records or establish the confidentiality of any previously exposed key and ciphertext.

The private-case service encrypts sensitive case payloads separately and checks current case access. Case IDs, assignments, workflow status and procedural audit metadata are not all encrypted fields. Deal tickets are separate: deal participants and authorized server staff can read them. Do not put confidential allegations in a deal ticket or public listing. Discord hosts messages, interactions and attachments under its own policies; an ephemeral response is private from other ordinary members, not from Discord or the bot.

Retention and deletion

Some workflow records remain longer than 30 days. There is currently no universal automatic 30-day deletion schedule. Records should be kept only while necessary for the described features, and removed when no longer needed. A listing's closure or expiry is not the same as deletion of its stored source snapshot.

When enabled, the VIP catalogue stores owner-submitted creator/resource/version and usage/support/claim information, authorization and review references, dependency references, applicable artifact hashes and offer expiry. Restricted release payloads use the existing application encryption keys, separate from public previews. Publisher IDs, revision hashes, publication states and public/private forum/thread IDs remain procedural metadata in SQLite. Only owner-authorized controls access restricted catalogue records; a staff role or VIP role does not grant that access. Partners receive no RDC member/VIP lists through this system. External creator sites process information members voluntarily give them under their own policies; an external link is not a new RDC data-sharing agreement.

The optional Resources reply stores only server/starter/reply IDs, timestamps, a stable delivery identity and bounded delivery states/error codes. It does not store poster profiles or resource message bodies. These identities prevent repeat advertisements after retries, deletion or restart. Release and delivery records are not exported as public dashboard/source content. There are no new behavioural analytics, third-party trackers or sales attribution.

VIP records have no automatic universal deletion deadline. The owner reviews necessity under the existing manual request/deletion process. Withdraw a release and verify removal of delivery links before erasing its restricted payload. Minimal post/delivery identities and revision/action metadata may remain while needed to prevent duplicate publication and recover safely. Payload erasure from the live database does not erase Discord messages or recovery/provider copies; the owner must account for those copies before restoration. Offer expiry or loss of VIP is not deletion of a licence, downloaded file or stored record. Necessary preservation holds and legal requests still follow the existing procedures.

News source-version storage is limited to 5,000 saved versions and 64 MiB of source payloads. There is no automatic time-based News deletion schedule: the owner must review records that are no longer needed. Reaching a storage limit holds new work for review rather than silently deleting history. These source limits do not cover every related delivery or audit record.

For the private-case service, the owner-approved deletion targets are 90 days after closure for case evidence and 365 days after closure for finding details. These targets apply only when the service, cleanup and deletion-safe recovery safeguards are enabled and verified. Active cases need manual review of what is still necessary. Open appeals and legal preservation holds pause deletion. A minimal record that a case or staff action happened may remain for accountability. These are not deletion deadlines for every older listing, application, moderation log, Discord message or provider copy. If cleanup or safe recovery is unavailable, the operator must address the delay and must not say the data has already been deleted.

To request access, correction, withdrawal of a published submission, or deletion of your bot-held data, email rdc.safety@proton.me. The RDC owner monitors this address, including when RU is offline or you cannot access the server. Discord DMs are optional, not required. Provide your Discord user ID and the relevant workflow/post identifiers; never send passwords or bot tokens. Identity may need to be verified before action. The mailbox provider processes the email you send.

The operator handles requests manually, including related workflow records/files and owner-held copies where applicable. Bot-held copies and Discord-hosted messages are distinct: removing one does not automatically erase the other. Recovery copies must be accounted for when fulfilling deletion, so restoring an old backup must not reintroduce deleted data.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit optional applications or listings and can request deletion of bot-held data. The bot currently has no general per-member switch to opt out of all automatic moderation or message processing in configured server channels. Do not submit sensitive personal information unnecessary for the feature.

Backups rotate independently of case deletion. Offline copies may remain until the operator refreshes or removes them. A current deletion ledger must be applied before restoring erased private-case data. Logical deletion does not promise instant physical erasure from storage, Discord, provider backups or copies held by other people. The operator must account for recovery copies and communicate any unresolved deletion limits rather than claiming every copy is gone.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected here and communicated through RDC where appropriate. Privacy questions and data requests go to the RDC owner, internetlord._, at rdc.safety@proton.me. RDC is operated in Michigan, United States. This address is not an emergency service.
